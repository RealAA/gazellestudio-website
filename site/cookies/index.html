Which cookies and browser storage the website and Gazelle Circle use, and why.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version

We don’t use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. The website sets no cookies at all. Gazelle Circle uses a few cookies that are strictly necessary to keep you signed in and to stop bots. Because nothing optional is used, there is no cookie banner.

On this website

No cookies. One item is kept in your browser’s session storage:

  • gs-project-finder: keeps the answers you’ve typed into the “Let’s talk” brief so they aren’t lost if you close the panel. It stays in the current tab only and is removed when the tab closes or the brief is sent.

In Gazelle Circle

  • sb-…-auth-token: keeps you signed in to your dashboard. Removed when you sign out; otherwise it can last up to 400 days.
  • gc_remember: remembers whether you ticked “Keep me signed in”. Lasts up to 400 days.
  • gc_tmp: when “Keep me signed in” is off, marks the session so it ends when you close the browser.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: on the join form, may store a short-lived value to check that you’re a person and not a bot.

Member cards in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet don’t use cookies.

Other sites

When you follow a link to WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn or Google, those sites may set their own cookies under their own policies.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block Gazelle Circle’s sign-in cookies, you won’t be able to stay signed in to your dashboard.
