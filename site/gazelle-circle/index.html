04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Good questions.

Do my customers need to download an app?+

No. Customers scan your QR code with their phone camera, join in seconds and get your member card straight away. They can save it to their home screen like an app.

How does the member card work?+

Your card is designed in your colours with your logo. Customers open it from their phone, and it updates whenever your team adds stamps or points.

How do the QR codes work?+

There are two. Your join QR (one per business) goes on your counter, menu or window so customers can sign up. Each customer then has their own QR on their card, which your team scans at the counter to add stamps or points.

Can my staff use it too?+

Yes. Add your team from the dashboard. Staff can scan cards and add stamps or points, managers can run campaigns and the card design, and owners control everything, including who has access.

What happens after I create my account?+

The Gazelle team reviews every business personally, usually within one working day. Once you’re approved, sign in to design your card, add your locations and print your QR code.

How much does it cost?+

Pricing depends on your business and setup. Create your account or message us on WhatsApp and we’ll talk you through the options.
