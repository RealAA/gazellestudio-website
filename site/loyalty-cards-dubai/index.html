Gazelle Circle is our loyalty platform for cafés, salons, barbers, clinics, gyms and shops in Dubai. Set it up once, then reward visits in seconds.

  • ✳︎

    QR sign-up

    Customers scan your QR code with their phone camera and join in seconds.

  • ✳︎

    Your card, your brand

    Choose stamps or points, your colours, your logo and the reward.

  • ✳︎

    No app to download

    The card opens on their phone and can be saved to the home screen.

  • ✳︎

    Scan and reward at the counter

HOW IT WORKS

A clear plan. Room for ideas.

01

Create your account

Sign up, add your logo and design your card. The Gazelle team reviews every business.

02

Download your join QR as a PNG, a print-ready SVG or an A4 poster.

03

Either. Stamp cards suit cafés, barbers and salons. Points suit restaurants and shops.
