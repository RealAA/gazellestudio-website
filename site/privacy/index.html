What we collect on this website and in Gazelle Circle, why, who helps us, and your rights.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

This notice explains how Gazelle Marketing Studio FZ-LLC (“Gazelle Studio”, “we”, “us”), a free zone company registered with the Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone (RAKEZ registration no. 0000004099962, services licence 47039211), CWIP1774, Compass Building, Al Hulaila Industrial Zone-FZ, Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates. Email info@gazellestudio.ae, phone +971 50 523 4793. handles personal data on gazellestudio.ae and in Gazelle Circle. We follow the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).

What we collect on this website

  • Project enquiries. When you send the contact form we receive your name, email, phone (if given), business, the services you picked and your message.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email. These links open your own app. Whatever you send reaches us through that service.
  • Technical data. Our host records basic request data such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Nothing else. The website has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools and sets no cookies. See the cookie policy.

Gazelle Circle

Gazelle Circle is our digital loyalty card platform for businesses.

  • Business accounts. When a business applies or signs in we collect the contact person’s name, email and phone, the business details and the team members it adds. For this data we are the controller.
  • Members of a business’s loyalty programme. When you join a business’s programme we collect your name, email, optional mobile number, your points and visits, and whether you agreed to receive offers. The business decides how its programme works and is the controller of this data; we process it on the business’s behalf. If you add your card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, Apple or Google stores the card on your device and account.
  • Offers by email or text. These are sent only if you ticked the box to receive them. Every message includes a way to stop them, and texts are only sent between 7am and 9pm UAE time.

Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a proposal you asked for.
  • To provide services and Gazelle Circle under our agreement with you or your business.
  • To send marketing messages, only with your consent.
  • To keep our systems secure and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We don’t sell personal data and we don’t use it for automated decisions that have legal or similar effects on you.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run the website and Gazelle Circle. They process data only on our instructions.

  • Netlify: website and app hosting (United States).
  • FormSubmit: delivers contact form enquiries and Gazelle Circle applications to our inbox.
  • Supabase: Gazelle Circle database and sign-in (servers in India).
  • Resend: email delivery for Gazelle Circle.
  • Twilio: text message delivery for Gazelle Circle.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: checks that sign-ups come from people, not bots.
  • Apple and Google: Wallet passes, when a member adds a card.
  • Anthropic: generates card design ideas from images a business chooses to upload in Card studio.
  • Google Maps: turns a map link a business pastes into a location.

We may also disclose data where UAE law or a competent authority requires it.

Transfers outside the UAE

Some of these providers store or process data outside the UAE, including in the United States, India and the European Union. We only use providers that commit by contract to protect personal data, and we limit what we send to what each service needs.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as we need them to reply and for our business records. Gazelle Circle data is kept while the business uses the platform. A member can ask the business, or us, to delete their card; a business can ask us to close its account and delete its data. We keep records longer only where UAE law requires it, for example invoices.

How we protect it

The website and Gazelle Circle are served only over HTTPS. Database access is restricted so that each business can see only its own data, and only through our server after a permission check. Service keys are encrypted and never sent to browsers. Sign-ups are rate-limited and checked for bots, and admin access is limited to Gazelle staff. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your data we will tell you and the UAE Data Office as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what data we hold about you, give you a copy, correct it, delete it, limit or stop how we use it, or move it to another provider. You can withdraw consent to marketing at any time. Email info@gazellestudio.ae; we aim to reply within 30 days. If you are a member of a business’s programme, you can also contact that business directly. If you’re not happy with our answer, you can complain to the UAE Data Office.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We don’t knowingly collect data from children under 18 without a parent’s or guardian’s consent.

Changes

We will update this notice when our practices change and show the new date at the top.
