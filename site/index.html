05 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Marketing in Dubai, answered.

What does a marketing agency in Dubai like Gazelle Studio do?+

We bring brand strategy, content production, social media, paid ads and lead generation, websites and SEO together for businesses in Dubai and across the UAE, so your marketing works as one plan instead of scattered pieces.

Do you work with small businesses and startups?+

Yes. Much of our work is with small and growing businesses. You can start with one focused project, like a website or a campaign, or a connected monthly plan.

Which industries do you work with?+

We’ve worked with 10+ brands internationally, including healthcare clinics and education businesses, producing 50+ videos and 150,000+ interactions. We welcome local brands in hospitality, beauty, retail and services. See marketing for clinics and marketing for cafés and restaurants.

Can you help my business show up on Google?+

Yes. Our SEO service covers technical SEO, on-page optimisation and local SEO, including your Google Business Profile, so people searching in Dubai can find you. See SEO & discovery.

How much does marketing cost in Dubai?+

It depends on the scope: a single campaign, a new website or an ongoing social media and ads plan. Tell us what you need and we’ll recommend a package that fits your goals and budget.

What is Gazelle Circle?+

Gazelle Circle is our branded membership and loyalty platform. Customers join with a QR code, collect stamps or points on every visit and keep the business’s digital member card on their phone, with no app to download. Explore Gazelle Circle.

Send a short brief through our contact page or message us on WhatsApp at +971 50 523 4793. We’ll reply to talk through your goals and next steps. Start a project.
