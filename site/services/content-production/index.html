Good questions.

Can production be part of social media management?+

Yes. Production can be combined with calendars, caption writing and publishing so the content has a plan beyond the shoot.

What is included in a shoot?+

That depends on the brief. The agreed scope sets the location, duration, number of deliverables, formats, editing and revisions. Talent, locations and other production extras are confirmed separately when needed.

Do you film on location in Dubai?+

Yes. Shoots can take place at your business or another agreed location in Dubai. Locations elsewhere in the UAE can be scoped for the brief.

How many videos can we get from one shoot?+

Often several. With a good plan, one shoot can produce a batch of short-form videos and photos for the weeks ahead. The exact number depends on the concepts, locations and editing agreed in the brief.

Can you edit footage we have already filmed?+

Yes. We can edit existing footage into short-form videos with captions, pacing and motion graphics, as long as the files are good enough to work with.
