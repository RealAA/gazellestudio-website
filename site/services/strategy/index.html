BRANDING IN DUBAI

STAND OUT
IN A CROWDED
MARKET.

Dubai is one of the most competitive places in the world to open a business. A new café, clinic or shop launches next to dozens offering something similar, to an audience from many countries and languages. Brand strategy decides what you say, who you say it to and why it should matter to them, before money goes into content, ads or a website.

We start with your customers and competitors, define the position you can own, then turn it into a voice, a visual identity and key messages your team can use every day. Everything else we make builds on that direction, from content production and social media to your website.

Good questions.

Can you work with our existing branding?+

Yes. We can build on an existing identity, refine what needs attention or develop a new direction. The scope depends on what your business needs.

Do you support local launches and collaborations?+

We can include promotion planning, partnership outreach and on-ground marketing support. The locations, responsibilities and deliverables are agreed for each campaign.

How long does a brand strategy project take?+

It depends on the scope. A focused positioning and messaging project is usually a matter of weeks, while a full identity with a logo, visual system and guidelines takes longer. We agree the timeline and deliverables before we start.

What do we receive at the end?+

A clear written direction covering your audience, positioning and key messages, plus the creative assets in the agreed scope, such as a visual identity, brand guidelines and campaign concepts your team and suppliers can use.

Is branding worth it for a small business?+

Yes, when it is sized to the business. A small business doesn’t need a long brand book, but it does need a clear reason for customers to choose it and a look people recognise. We scope the work to what will actually be used.
