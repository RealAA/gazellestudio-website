Good questions.

Do you guarantee a number of leads?+

No. Results depend on the offer, audience, competition, advertising budget, website and follow-up. We agree useful measures and report on actual campaign performance.

Can you improve the page people land on?+

Yes. Landing-page creative and enquiry or booking flows can be included. This is scoped alongside the campaign so the ad and the destination work together.

Should we use Google Ads or Meta Ads?+

It depends on how people look for you. Google Ads reach people already searching for a service, which suits clinics and other needs people search for. Meta Ads on Instagram and Facebook create demand with visuals, which suits restaurants, retail and launches. Many businesses use both.

How much should we spend on ads?+

There is no single number. It depends on your market, your goals and how competitive your searches are in Dubai. We recommend a starting budget based on your goals, then adjust it as the data comes in. Ad spend is agreed separately from our fee.

How quickly will we see enquiries?+

Campaigns can start bringing traffic soon after launch, but the first weeks are usually a learning period while we test audiences, messages and pages. We report on actual performance and refine from there.
