ABOUT US

A MARKETING
AGENCY IN
DUBAI.

Gazelle Studio is an independent marketing agency serving Dubai and the UAE. We bring brand strategy, content production, social media, paid ads, websites and SEO together, so small and growing businesses get one connected plan instead of a handful of disconnected suppliers.

We have worked with 10+ brands, including Palmcare Clinic in Liwan, House of Ilm and Paradise Plus Clinic, producing 50+ videos, earning 150,000+ interactions and winning top search rankings for a client’s key terms.

Gazelle Studio is the trading name of Gazelle Marketing Studio FZ-LLC, licensed by RAKEZ. We also build Gazelle Circle, our digital loyalty card platform for businesses in Dubai.

OUR MISSION

One team for your marketing.

Small and growing businesses usually end up juggling a designer, a videographer, an ads freelancer and a web developer who never talk to each other. We want to replace that with one team, one plan and one invoice, and to make work that is clear about what it is for and looks like nobody else’s.

OUR STORY

Registered in October 2026.

Gazelle Marketing Studio FZ-LLC was registered with the Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone (RAKEZ) on 27 October 2026. It holds a RAKEZ services licence for marketing management, social media management and software design, and a media licence for still photography. We work with businesses in Dubai and across the UAE, and we build Gazelle Circle, our own digital loyalty card platform, alongside client work.
