WHO WE ARE

A MARKETING
AGENCY IN
DUBAI.

Gazelle Studio is an independent marketing agency serving Dubai and the UAE. We bring brand strategy, content production, social media, paid ads, websites and SEO together, so small and growing businesses get one connected plan instead of a handful of disconnected suppliers.

We have worked with 10+ brands, including Palmcare Clinic in Liwan, House of Ilm and Paradise Plus Clinic, producing 50+ videos, earning 150,000+ interactions and winning top search rankings for a client’s key terms.

Gazelle Studio is the trading name of Gazelle Marketing Studio FZ-LLC, licensed by RAKEZ. We also build Gazelle Circle, our digital loyalty card platform for businesses in Dubai.
